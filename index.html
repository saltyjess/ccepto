Book Fair

Fall Book Fair Preview Week — Oct 19th–23rd

Students preview the book fair with their class this week and fill out their wish lists. Ask your kiddo what’s on theirs!

Book Fair

Fall Book Fair — Oct 26th–30th

Students shop before school and during their class’s time slot. Families can shop together at Family Fun Night on Tuesday, Oct 27th from 4:00–6:00 PM.

Family Fun Night

Family Fun Night — Tuesday Oct 27th @ CCE 4:00–6:00 PM

Shop the book fair! Plus a dunk tank, pumpkin bowling, inflatables, and food truck favorites: Potbelly BBQ, Jeremiah’s Frozen Ice, and Dean and Luca.

A little snack. A big difference. Donate snacks or canned food for a turn at the dunk tank! Individually wrapped pretzels, Cheez-Its, Goldfish, granola bars (no peanut butter), cereal boxes, Pop-Tarts, fruit snacks, applesauce pouches, yogurt pouches (must be shelf stable), or breakfast bars, plus canned soup, vegetables, chili, or Chef Boyardee.
